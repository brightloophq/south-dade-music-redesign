## 2. Audience

**Primary — parents considering music education for a child.** What they want is
not musical skill; it is evidence their child can do a hard thing in front of
people. Their real objection is not price, it is *"my child is too shy for
this."*

**Secondary — teenagers**, who must find the academy **inspiring rather than
childish**. A teenager embarrassed by the site will veto the parent. This is the
strongest argument against the rainbow-and-cartoon idiom of the category.

⚠️ A large portion of families are Spanish-speaking. The site claims full
bilingual support and delivers none (gate B-6).

### The unlock

> **Nobody's face appears on the homepage. Ever. That is the concept, not the
> compromise.**

Permitted: the **back** of a small head in light · a **silhouette** against a
lit scrim · **hands** on keys · a **shoulder** in the wings · the audience as
**dark shapes** · a parent's **hands** in a lap · a **shadow** crossing a floor.

You become her — a face is someone else's child, a back is your own. It is
universal. It is how cinema builds dread and release. And it is ethically
bulletproof: **a shoot on this principle needs no consent forms.**

## 15. Current engineering architecture

**Next.js 16.3 (App Router, Turbopack) · React 19.2 · TypeScript 5 strict ·
Tailwind CSS 4.3 · GSAP 3.15 · Lenis · Storybook 10.5.**

```
src/app/  src/components/{ui,layout,home,motion}/  src/lib/motion/
src/tokens/  src/config/  src/content/  src/styles/
```

**The token layer is the swap point.** Colour, type, spacing, radius, elevation
and motion all resolve through `src/tokens/` into CSS custom properties;
components reference semantic aliases, never raw values.

**A new visual language expressed as tokens propagates without touching a single
component.** New section order or layout primitives do require component work.

## 16. Available GSAP and motion capabilities

GSAP 3.15 with **ScrollTrigger, SplitText, Flip, CustomEase**; `matchMedia()`,
`context()`, `quickSetter()`, `quickTo()`. **Lenis** drives ScrollTrigger with
no competing loops. Canvas 2D for texture. **No WebGL** — nothing here needs it.

**Seven live timelines**, 19 ScrollTrigger instances: `GradeTimeline` (colour
grade, scrubbed) · `HeroTimeline` (0.8) · `WalkTimeline` (0.9) ·
`JourneyTimeline` (0.7, **pinned**) · `FirstNoteTimeline` · `FinaleTimeline` (1)
· `EmergeBatch`.

**The light is a character.** One source, seven moods (`wait · search · follow ·
hesitate · explode · disperse · shaft`), exposed as `--light-x`, `--light-y`,
`--light-intensity`, `--light-flare`, `--atmos-tint`, `--vignette`. Everything
is lit by it.

**Camera:** push · pull · plane · rackFocus · breathe. ❌ Never shake, spin or
rotate unnecessarily.

**Budget by template:** home/flagship/performances **high** · camps/detail
medium · instrument/teachers low · scholarships/pricing minimal ·
**faq/contact none**.

**Pinning rationed:** max **2 sitewide**, ≤ 300vh each, desktop only.

❌ **Never animated:** prices at decision · deposit amounts · refund terms ·
policy text · camp dates · capacity · guarantee text · contact details · errors
· focus indicators · skip links · safeguarding information.

❌ **Prohibited outright:** auto-advancing carousels · scroll-jacking · forced
snapping · wheel hijacking · cursor followers · custom cursors · preloaders ·
text scramble · typewriter headings · infinite loops during reading · background
video autoplay on mobile · animated student photography · confetti and sparkles
· horizontal scroll · parallax on text · sticky CTA during the film.

❌ **Forbidden reveal vocabulary: fade up · slide up · stagger.** Banned by name.
Reveal by light, mask, clip-path or camera instead.

## 17. Accessibility requirements

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| Body contrast | **4.5:1 minimum**, AAA where achievable |
| Large text / UI | 3:1 minimum |
| Touch targets | **44 × 44px**, every control |
| Body text | **16px minimum on every device** |
| Focus | Always visible, never removed, never animated |
| Motion | `prefers-reduced-motion` + user-facing override (`sdm:reduced-motion`) |
| Colour | Never the sole carrier of meaning |
| Headings | Semantic order, never chosen for size |

Already built: focus management on route change, a focus trap in the mobile
drawer (and deliberately **not** in the desktop menu — a menu is not a dialog),
skip links, 44px targets throughout.

**Reduced motion gets a parallel cut, not a degraded one:** the same narrative
and the same colour arc, delivered static. Every scroll-driven moment needs a
static equivalent carrying the same meaning.

⚠️ The live site has no accessibility statement and real defects — placeholder
alt text, filename-as-alt, missing alt on all six gallery images, H2 misuse.
⚠️ No physical accessibility information exists for the premises.

## 18. Performance requirements

60fps target, **50fps floor**, ≤ 50ms main-thread block. Fonts self-hosted with
**zero CDN requests**. GSAP dynamically imported off the critical path. Images
through `next/image` with correct aspect ratios.

Motion degrades on genuinely low-end hardware (`cores ≤ 2` or `memory ≤ 2`GB).

> Chrome quantizes `deviceMemory` and caps it at 8. An earlier gate of
> `cores ≤ 4 || memory ≤ 4` silently disabled scrub, pinning and parallax on
> ordinary laptops. Detection is deliberately generous now.

**Typographic implication:** every added face, weight and style is a real
download. Variable fonts with only the axes you use are strongly preferred.

⚠️ Gate M-4 — the P75 device profile has never been confirmed from analytics.

## 20. The creative concept — "The Walk"

**Confirmed. Not in scope for redesign.**

> **The homepage is the walk from the wings to the microphone, in the dark.**
> **The scroll is the walk. You are the child.**

You arrive backstage. Black. You hear a room through a wall — muffled, full of
people. You start to move, and the scroll becomes footsteps on a wooden stage.
Light grows from one edge. You reach your mark. Everything stops. And then a
note.

**There are no sections. There is a building, and you walk through it.**

### Why this earns the ambition

Most award-bait sites impose a cinematic metaphor on a business that has nothing
to do with it. **This business's core experience literally is this walk.** The
90-Day Stage Program exists to produce it. The Gradual Exposure Ladder is a
method for surviving it. The guarantee is a promise about it.

We are not dressing a music school as a film. We are showing the thing the
school actually sells.

### The visitor should feel anticipation, tension, and release.

Reference frame: **A24** (withholding — let the frame be dark, never explain the
shot) · **Apple** (scroll as a directed camera, obsessive restraint, one idea
per screen) · **Nike** (physical POV — you are inside the body, not watching it)
· **MasterClass** (cinematic top, conventional close: sell the feeling,
transact in the light) · **Broadway** (the house, the hush, the note).

Anti-reference: every scroll-jacked agency site with a custom cursor and a
loading percentage. Craft here is load-bearing, never decoration.

## 21. Proposed typography direction — Archivo + Newsreader

**A recommendation from the internal visual-direction pass, for the design
process to accept, refine or reject** — see `docs/design/visual-direction.md`.

### The problem

**Inter is the native typeface of the dialect this brief prohibits.** It is the
face of Vercel, Linear and Tailwind's own documentation. It was drawn, superbly,
to be invisible in software — and invisibility is the one quality unaffordable
in a page whose stated job is to carry the experience through typography.

Nobody will say *"that's Inter."* They will say **"this looks like an app."**

### The proposal

| Role | Face | Axes | Carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Structure** | **Archivo** | `wght 100–900`, `wdth 62–125` | Navigation, labels, data, the one shout |
| **Voice** | **Newsreader** | `opsz 6–72`, `wght 200–800`, roman + *italic* | Whispered in-frame lines, pull quotes, all reading copy |

**Archivo stays** — already committed, real width axis, reads institutional
rather than corporate. **Newsreader arrives** — a variable serif with an
optical-size axis and an expressive italic. At 20px in the dark it reads as a
film subtitle or a programme note in your hands.

The pairing is **grotesque for the building, serif for the person inside it** —
what performing-arts institutions look like, and not what software looks like.

⚠️ Newsreader is open-licence. With budget for a commercial text serif the
direction improves; without it, nothing is compromised. Must ship `latin` **and**
`latin-ext` for Spanish.

**Cost:** a token edit plus two font files. No component changes.

## 22. The one-shout rule

> **The typographic idea is the distance between a whisper and a shout — and how
> rarely we shout.**

| Role | Size | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **Whisper** — serif italic | 20px | ~12 × |
| **Statement** — grotesque semibold | 40px | **2 ×** |
| **THE SHOUT** — grotesque bold, wide | 180px | **1 × — once on the page** |
| **Programme** — serif roman | 17px | throughout |

**The current build uses display type in the hero and four more places. Five
shouts is no shouts.** One word on the whole homepage is set large, and it lands
because nothing else competes.

Supporting rules: nothing centred until the house lights come up · never more
than eight words in frame during the dark acts · measure 62–68 characters ·
italic is a *voice*, not an emphasis.

⚠️ The shout word itself is **unwritten**. It is the largest type on the site and
needs a copy decision from the owner, not a design one.

## 23. Amber is light, not decoration

> **Amber appears four times on the entire homepage.**

1. The seam of light under the door
2. The source that lights the walk
3. The flash at the moment of release
4. The primary CTA fill

**That is the whole budget.** It is not a border colour, not a hover state, not
an icon tint, not a chip.

The current palette calls the accent "Spotlight" and then spends it like a brand
colour. When the light is everywhere, none of it reads as light — and the
moments that should be events become just more amber.

⚠️ Contrast: **amber as text on a light ground fails.** It works as a fill with
dark text. That is how the CTA achieves 9.70:1.

## 24. Composition laws

The brief's prohibitions are **compositional, not chromatic.** A dark page with
beautiful type still looks like a Tailwind site if it is built from a grid of
rounded cards. These are structural bans, each checkable in review.

| Law | Why |
|---|---|
| **Border radius is 0** — one exception, the CTA pill | Radius is the strongest component-library tell there is |
| **No box shadows, ever** | Depth comes only from the light. A shadow that does not agree with the light is a lie about the room. |
| **No cards** — no bordered, filled or elevated containers | The "grid of rounded cards" prohibition, made enforceable |
| **No generic icons** | Icon rows are the most template-coded element on the web |
| **Asymmetric measures** — content in 5–7 of 12 columns, offset from centre | Centred columns read as a document template |
| **Acts separated by full-bleed temperature shifts**, never rules or cards | The page changes *rooms*; it does not stack *sections* |
| **One idea per screen** in the dark acts | Density is the enemy of anticipation |
| **Letterbox 2.39:1** during the film, opening at the release | Establishes that you are watching — then that you have arrived |

Phase 5D already removed 47 rounded cards and 24 shadows from the page body.
These laws exist to stop them coming back.

### Colour as an arc, not a palette

The decisive idea: **the neutrals carry the story.** Backstage is blue-biased
black — cold, nervous, before. Memory is brown-biased black — warm, past. The
house is ivory. Nobody names these; everybody feels that the past is a different
temperature from the stage.

### Light and grain

One source, and **the type is lit by it** — glyph brightness follows the light.
Sub-perceptual, and it is the difference between words *on the glass* and words
*in the room*.

**One animated grain layer at 3% opacity over everything, including type.** The
cheapest item in this document and the largest single contributor to "this feels
like film." Without it, dark gradients band and the page reads digital.
