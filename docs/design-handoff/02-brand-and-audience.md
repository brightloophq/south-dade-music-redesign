# 02 — Brand and Audience

---

## 1. Brand name — unresolved

⚠️ **Gate B-5.** Four names run concurrently across the live estate:

| Name | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| **South Dade Music** | Sitewide footer copyright, `og:site_name` on every page |
| **South Dade Music Academy** | Lead-gen subdomain title and body, `/singing-lessons/`, four testimonials |
| **SDMA** | Lead-gen subdomain only |
| **South Dade Music LLC** | Subdomain footer — the only legal-entity reference anywhere |

This is the **first decision the owner must make.** It blocks the logo lockup,
the wordmark, structured data, ™ usage and domain strategy.

**For design purposes:** use **South Dade Music**. It has the strongest
corroboration (sitewide footer plus metadata). Do not design a lockup that
depends on the longer name until B-5 closes.

**™ status:** "90-Day Stage Program™" carries the mark in the sitewide footer
but *not* on the program page itself. Do not set the ™ in any comp until B-5
resolves whether the mark is registered.

## 4. Core positioning for the redesign

The strongest asset is not the lessons. It is the **transformation**: a nervous
beginner becomes a child confident enough to perform live, in ninety days.

Seven qualities the homepage must carry:

**anticipation · courage · transformation · performance · belonging ·
professionalism · emotional payoff**

## 5. Audience

### Primary — parents considering music education for a child

What they actually want is not musical skill. It is evidence that their child
can do a hard thing in front of people. The 90-Day Stage Program is the only
offer on the market that names that outcome and dates it.

Their real objection is not price. It is *"my child is too shy for this."* The
Gradual Exposure Ladder exists to answer exactly that, and the design should
make the answer feel safe rather than merely stated.

### Secondary — teenagers

They must find the academy **inspiring rather than childish.** A teenager who
feels the site was made for small children will not enrol, and will veto a
parent who wants them to.

This is the single strongest argument against the rainbow-and-cartoon idiom that
dominates the category, and the strongest argument for a cinematic, adult,
performing-arts treatment that a fifteen-year-old would not be embarrassed by.

### Demographic reality

✅ Service area, identical sentence on five pages:

> We serve families in Florida City, Homestead, Cutler Bay, Palmetto Bay, and
> nearby areas.

⚠️ **Language — gate B-6.** The site claims *"A large portion of our families
are Spanish-speaking… We offer full bilingual support across our core programs
and enrollment processes."*

**This claim is unsupported.** There is no Spanish version of any page, no
language switcher, no `hreflang`. One testimonial is in Spanish. Either the
bilingual experience gets built or the claim gets dropped — the design should
assume Spanish is real and plan for it, because the demographic clearly is.

**Design implication:** Spanish runs roughly 15–25% longer than English. Any
display type set tight to a measure must survive that. Spanish body copy is
already specified at line-height 1.7 in the token layer, because accented
ascenders need the room.

## 6. Ages served — conflicted

| Range | Context | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **3–18** | primary market focus | `/resources/`, homepage FAQ |
| **3–6** | early childhood | `/early-childhood/` |
| **adults** | beginner / returning | `/private-lessons/`, `/programs/` |
| **7–15** | summer camp — a hard requirement | both camp pages |
| **6 and up** | group lessons | `/group-music-lessons/` |
| **age 3** | youngest starting age | subdomain |

✅ The homepage currently uses **"Ages 3–18. Adults welcome."** — the safest
formulation, and verbatim-supported.

## 7. Tone

**Is:** calm, exact, warm without being sentimental, confident without
overclaiming. The voice of a good programme note.

**Is not:** peppy, exclamatory, keyword-stuffed, or reassuring in the way that
signals there is something to be reassured about.

The single most persuasive sentence written for this project is authored, not
extracted, and shows the register:

> That's not shyness. That's a skill she hasn't been taught yet.

It moves the problem from the child's character to a teachable gap. That is the
move that makes everything else purchasable.

## 8. What the brand is not

❌ Not faith-affiliated — ⚠️ gate **B-1** is open and unanswered. Design nothing
that implies either a religious or a pointedly secular identity until it closes.

❌ Not a franchise, not a chain, not a conservatory. A local academy whose
teachers are known by name to families and named nowhere on its own website
(⚠️ gate **B-7**).
