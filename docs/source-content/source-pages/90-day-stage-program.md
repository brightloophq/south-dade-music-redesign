## Main body copy (verbatim highlights)

> At South Dade Music, our **90-Day Stage Program** is designed to help students go from learning to performing in a clear and simple way. The goal is not just to practice, but to get ready for the stage in a short and focused time.

> This program is a step-by-step plan that helps students prepare for a live performance in about three months.

In this program, students: Choose songs to work on · Practice with a clear goal · Improve their skills each week · Get ready to perform in front of others

**Why Stage Experience Matters**
> Programs that include performance help students grow faster and feel more connected to music. In fact, music programs that offer real performance opportunities help students develop confidence and stronger skills over time.

**3-Step Journey:** Step 1 "students start by working on their chosen songs… timing, technique, and understanding the music." Step 2 "students begin practicing like they are on stage… playing full songs, working on mistakes, and improving consistency." Step 3 "students prepare for a real performance. They practice confidence, stage presence, and staying calm while playing."

**Build Confidence:** Stay focused while playing · Handle small mistakes · Keep going without stopping · Feel comfortable in front of an audience

**Learn Real Performance Skills:** How to prepare for a performance · How to practice effectively · How to stay on rhythm and timing · How to play with expression and energy

**Who Is This Program For?** Students already taking lessons · Beginners who want a clear goal · Kids and teens who want to perform · Students who need help building confidence

**A Real Performance at the End**
> At the end of the 90 days, students get the chance to perform. This is the highlight of the program.

**Local:** "We serve families in Florida City, Homestead, Cutler Bay, Palmetto Bay, and nearby areas."
