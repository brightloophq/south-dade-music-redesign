## FAQs (verbatim — this is the site's canonical FAQ set)

**What ages do you teach?**
> South Dade Music provides private and group music instruction for children, teens, and adults. Our primary market focus is families with children ages 3–18, including early-childhood programs for ages 3–6, as well as adults seeking beginner or returning instruction.

**Do you accept Step Up scholarships?**
> Yes, South Dade Music partners with Step Up for Students as an approved provider. We accept PEP (Personalized Education Program) and UA (Unique Abilities) scholarships. Please note that these scholarships are administered and awarded by Step Up for Students, not by South Dade Music.

**Are lessons available in Spanish?**
> Yes. A large portion of our families are Spanish-speaking, so providing an effective, parent-friendly Spanish language experience that improves trust and connection is a high priority. We offer full bilingual support across our core programs and enrollment processes so Spanish-only parents have complete clarity.

**Do students perform live?**
> Yes. We actively engage in community outreach through free performances and collaborative ensembles like choirs, orchestras, and modern band. Our core framework is built around a live showcase, and every student performs in a live showcase within 90 days.

**Is this just regular music lessons?**
> No. This is a performance program. Students attend two classes weekly: one for skill, one for band application.

**What if my child is too shy?**
> That is exactly what we train for. We use a Gradual Exposure Ladder to move them from isolation to community safely.

**How does the trial work?**
> It is a 90-Day Stage Experience Preview. We hold your spot for $25, which is credited to your tuition upon enrollment. This preview is backed by our Stage-Ready Guarantee: if your child is not prepared to step on stage at the end of the 90-day cycle, we will continue coaching them at no additional charge until they are ready.
