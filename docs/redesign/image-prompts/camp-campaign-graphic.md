# Camp campaign graphic

**Use:** the *background layer* of a camp campaign card or social graphic.
**Aspect:** `1:1` and `4:5` social, `16:9` web band.
**Register:** decorative.

> ⚠️ **This is not the camp selling image.** `09-image-strategy.md` §4 bars AI
> from the camp selling image outright — a parent is buying supervised care for
> their child on the strength of it, so that image must be a real, current-year
> photograph. This template produces a *ground* that real photography and type
> sit on top of. If a layout uses this alone as the camp's primary image, the
> layout is wrong.

## Requirements

- Brighter and warmer than the stage register — camp is the **community**
  register (`04-design-system.md` §13): daylight, not spotlight.
- Leave a clear centre zone for the date, price and CTA.
- No depiction of a camp, a room, an activity, or anything happening.

```prompt
A bright, warm, summery abstract background for a seasonal campaign graphic. Sunlit golden and amber tones (#F5A524, #FFD68A) washing across the frame with soft daylight bloom, warm cream (#FAF9F7) opening up through the centre, and a gentle deep-teal shadow gathering at the lower corners. Soft geometric bands and light leaks suggesting an open, energetic summer day. Uncluttered centre so headline type and a call-to-action can sit over it cleanly. Optimistic and open, restrained saturation, no clutter. Purely abstract colour and light.
```
