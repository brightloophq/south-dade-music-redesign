# Hero atmospheric background

**Use:** the dark ground behind a page H1, where a real photograph is not yet
available or where the hero is type-led rather than photograph-led.
**Aspect:** `16:9` primary. Generate `4:5` separately for the mobile crop.
**Register:** decorative. Never presented as a photograph of a venue.

## Requirements

- Must carry white overlaid type at AA contrast — keep the upper-left third
  dark and low-detail, per `04-design-system.md` §2.
- Must survive the light-sweep animation in `05-motion-system.md` §9, so no
  competing horizontal highlight across the centre band.
- Deep shadows must retain detail; no crushed black gradients that band.

Restrained saturation, warm highlights against blue-black shadows, film-grade tonality with detail retained in the shadows. Non-representational, purely atmospheric light and colour.
