# Stage-light texture

**Use:** section dividers, band backgrounds, the ground behind the 90-Day
Timeline and Exposure Ladder diagrams. Explicitly permitted in
`09-image-strategy.md` §4.
**Aspect:** `21:9` for dividers, `16:9` for full-bleed bands.
**Register:** decorative.

## Requirements

- Non-representational. Beams and haze only — no stage, no rigging, no
  instruments, no venue architecture.
- Tileable-feeling edges: avoid a strong single focal point that breaks when
  the band is cropped at different viewport widths.
- Sits *behind* content, so contrast range must stay narrow enough that
  foreground text remains readable.

```prompt
An abstract stage-lighting texture. Narrow beams of warm amber light (#F5A524, #FFC15C) cutting at a shallow angle through dark theatrical haze against a deep blue-black ground (#070A12). Volumetric shafts with soft falloff, dust motes catching the light, gentle chromatic bloom at the beam edges. A faint deep crimson (#8B1E3F) undertone in the darkest regions suggesting curtain velvet without depicting fabric. Even distribution across the frame with no single dominant focal point. Restrained, elegant, cinematic. Purely abstract light and atmosphere.
```
