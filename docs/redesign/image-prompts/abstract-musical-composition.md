# Abstract musical composition

**Use:** editorial breaks, card backgrounds, open-graph grounds — anywhere the
page needs to read as *music* without claiming to show a lesson.
**Aspect:** `3:2` editorial, `4:3` card, `1:1` social.
**Register:** decorative.

## Requirements

- Waveforms, resonance, harmonic geometry — **not** musical notation, and
  **not** floating note glyphs. `04-design-system.md` §14 bans note confetti.
- No instrument that reads as a product shot standing in for a page hero.
- Geometric and restrained; no 3D renders, no glossy plastic surfaces.

```prompt
An abstract visual composition evoking sound and resonance. Flowing waveform ridges and concentric harmonic rings rendered as fine single-weight lines and soft gradient fields, layered over a deep blue-black ground (#0D1220). Warm amber (#FFC15C) and muted crimson (#8B1E3F) picking out the leading edges of the forms, fading into darkness at the frame edges. A sense of vibration and standing waves in still air. Geometric, restrained, elegant, editorial. No musical notation, no note symbols, no staves. Purely abstract form and light.
```
